Fast-calculation tricks

Multiply by 11

Outer digits stay; insert the sum of adjacent digits (carry if >9).

e.g.
86 × 11
8|(8+6=14)|6 → carry → 946
Square ending in 5

n5² = n(n+1) | 25.

e.g.
65²
6×7=42 | 25 → 4225
Square near 50

(50±x)² = (25±x) hundreds, then x².

e.g.
48²
23 | 04 → 2304
Square near 100

(100−x)² = (100−2x) | x² (2-digit block).

e.g.
96²
92 | 16 → 9216
Cross-multiply (close 2-digit)

units×units, cross-add, tens×tens; carry left.

e.g.
23 × 21
4 | 8 | 3 → 483
× 5 / × 25 / × 125

÷2×10 · ÷4×100 · ÷8×1000.

e.g.
64 × 25
64÷4 ×100 → 1600
× 9 / × 99

×10−itself · ×100−itself.

e.g.
56 × 99
5600−56 → 5544
Percentage swap

x% of y = y% of x.

e.g.
18% of 250
250% of 18 → 45
